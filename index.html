Leadership · Convening

The Council of Queens

A research and editorial platform examining beauty histories, hair traditions, representation, fashion, photography, consumer behaviour and beauty economics.

Could become → Research · Media · Partnerships
Enterprise · Networks

African Queen Women in Enterprise

A founder-intelligence property spanning profiles, directories, mentorship, research, investor networks, interviews and a possible entrepreneurship fellowship.

Could become → Network · Summit · Accelerator
